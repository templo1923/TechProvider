FAQ

Do I need business verification?⌄

No. config_id 1889600718898450 is a Tech Provider config approved in production. When your Meta app is in dev mode and you initiate Embedded Signup, Meta does not require Business Verification for the buyer to connect. Perfect for testing and for reselling flow.

Will my messages go through you?⌄

Never. Once override_callback_uri succeeds, Meta sends events directly to your destination_url. If override fails due to permissions, we still drop messages in our router and forward nothing but statuses. Message-blind by architecture.

What about Coexistence with WhatsApp Business App?⌄

Check the box. We launch FB.login with setup: {businessAppCoexistence:true}. Your number stays usable on the phone app plus Cloud API. History is kept on phone. If you uncheck, it fully migrates.

What does TechProvider Seller store?⌄

Only waba_id, phone_number_id, destination_url, encrypted token (AES-256-GCM), and message statuses. No message bodies. Isolated per tenant via RLS logic in Upstash keys: waba_map:phone_id and logs:phone_id.

What are common blockers before launch?⌄

Payment method already exists on WABA (delete it in Business Manager), number not eligible for Cloud (must be able to receive SMS/voice), chat history backup prompt (disable if migrating). We list these in the modal.

How does pricing work vs Meta billing?⌄

We charge flat per connections. Meta charges per conversation (marketing, utility, auth, service) directly to the buyer’s payment method attached to their WABA. We never meter, toll, or markup messages. Official model.
